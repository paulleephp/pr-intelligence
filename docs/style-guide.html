PR Intelligence Style Guide

Colors and brand mark. Every color is derived from two anchors on the home page: the emerald → cyan title gradient and the emerald Analyze button. Rules and exact values are in STYLEGUIDE.md; this page shows them.

Brand mark

"Diff lines": three diff lines, the middle one carrying a finding marker, in dark teal on the title gradient. It says "reads your diff and flags things". The gradient carries the theme, so the wordmark is plain near-white. File: docs/brand/mark.svg.

How the colors are derived

1 · AnchorTitle gradient emerald-400 (hue 163°) to cyan-400 (212°). Midpoint 187° teal, lightness 0.785, chroma 0.144 in OKLCH.
2 · ComplementTeal's complement is 7°. Severities sit on the warm arc next to it (15°, 50°, 80°); low (232°) is analogous to the theme.
3 · Lightness rampCritical darkest and most saturated, medium lightest, so levels stay distinct with red-green color blindness.

Severity

Outline badges: colored border, dot and text, no tinted fill (orange tinted over near-black renders as brown). Critical alone is a solid badge, as its own level.
